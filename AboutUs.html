A Vision in action

Our Story

This initiative began in July of 2019 as small pieces of a puzzle in the minds of three young ladies with diverse backgrounds, interests, and passions. They were all living separate lives, trying to make a difference in whatever small ways they could. When they crossed paths, the world they imagined and desired to create drew them together. Even though they valued individuality, they knew that the community was far superior, so they banded together and founded CHICHASA. The name is composed of the initial letters of their respective names. Why? This is because it highlights individual interests that influence the organization's overall direction.

CHI: This represents Chisunta Chikwamu (5th from left). She was born as the only girl in a family of three in Kabwe, Zambia. In 2018, she served as Headgirl for the board of prefects at Fatima Girls' secondary school, where she excelled in all academic disciplines and was an outstanding student. In addition, she was a member of the Red Cross Association, Anti-Corruption Commission, and Public Speaking. She improved her communication, public speaking, and advocacy skills as a result of her involvement in these clubs. Her journey toward justice and peace began here. Chisunta is concerned with equal opportunities for all people, regardless of identity, age, gender, or race. The majority of her interests are based on a feminist worldview.

CHA: This represents Chanda Leo (4th from left). She was born in Kitwe, Zambia. She then attended Mpelembe Secondary School. Due to her love and concern for the environment, she adopted a vegan lifestyle. diet. Despite being told multiple times that her diet was a performance, she remains vegan eight years later. Through her diet, she saw the benefits of a healthy lifestyle and switched entirely to organic products. After experiencing the benefits of this life, she desired a similar life for others, which sparked her interest in medicine. Chanda desires a world in which everyone has access to sustainable and high-quality healthcare.

SA: This represents Samantha Chizalema (4th from left). She was born in Lusaka, Zambia. In 2017, she served as Headgirl for the board of prefects at Ibenga Girls' secondary school, where she excelled in all academic subjects and was an outstanding student. Samatha was a member of the Zambia Robotics Team and has served as a leader on numerous teams. Her actions are motivated by her calm and collected demeanor. As a result of her leadership and concern for the environment, she helmed a number of successful ventures, including a sustainable sole fashion design business.

It can be said that fate brought the three ladies together and through meaningful conversations and a strong conviction to do something about the wrongs they had witnessed, including injustices to those in need and environmental degradation, in their society and the world at large, they decided to begin a movement towards human and environmental well-being.

Who we are

We are a youth-led organization that seeks to educate the general public on the significance of human development and environmental awareness by providing quality services through volunteer work. We seek meaningful partnerships that foster the creation of a society in which everyone is free, safe, and has access to opportunities for quality growth in order to secure the future for future generations. The goal is to create a world in which the most disadvantaged (women and children) have access to quality healthcare, education, and equal opportunity.
